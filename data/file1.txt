The Mars Colony Alpha was established in 2042 by the United Space Coalition. Its primary energy source is the Helios-3 Fusion Reactor, which outputs 500 megawatts of power. The colony currently houses 1,200 residents, primarily scientists and engineers. Agriculture is handled in the Green Dome, utilizing hydroponic systems to grow soy, wheat, and algae. The atmosphere inside the dome is maintained at 21 degrees Celsius with 40% humidity. Emergency protocols dictate that in the event of a depressurization, all personnel must gather in Sector 4 within 5 minutes. The colony commander is Captain Sarah Jenkins, who previously piloted the Starship Voyager.
